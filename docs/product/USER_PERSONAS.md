# User Personas

Seven users. All are served by the same public pages — there is no separate expert tool.
When their needs conflict, the resident wins.

---

## 1. Resident

**Goals.** Understand whether the neighborhood is getting safer or less safe, without
becoming a data analyst. Decide ordinary things: whether to walk home that way, whether to
go to a meeting.

**Questions.** Is crime up or down here? What kind? Is my block actually different from the
rest of the neighborhood? Is this a trend or a bad week?

**Needs.** Neighborhood Overview and Crime Trends with a plainly stated direction and
period. A map that does not pretend to more precision than exists. Plain language, no
jargon, no unexplained rates.

---

## 2. Small business owner with repeated work-vehicle break-ins

**Goals.** Stop losing tools and vans. Find out whether the problem is theirs alone or
neighborhood-wide, and get someone to act.

**Questions.** How many break-ins like mine happened near here, and when? Is it rising? Who
do I actually call — police, the alderman, the city? Has anyone promised to do something?

**Needs.** Filter to a specific crime category and a small area over time. A clear
"Who Is Responsible" answer that distinguishes who can *do* something from who can only
*ask*. A printable summary to bring to a beat meeting. This persona is the worked example
in [ACCOUNTABILITY_FRAMEWORK.md](../methodology/ACCOUNTABILITY_FRAMEWORK.md).

---

## 3. CAPS / beat-meeting attendee

**Goals.** Show up with facts rather than anecdotes, and hold the meeting to specifics.

**Questions.** What changed in this beat since the last meeting? What did the commander
commit to last time? Did it happen?

**Needs.** The **Beat Meeting Brief** — one page, printable, current, with source and
refresh date on it. Commitment tracking with evidence and honest `unclear` statuses.

---

## 4. Nextdoor community lead

**Goals.** Counter rumor with something checkable. Share a summary that does not inflame.

**Questions.** Is the thing everyone is posting about real? What is the actual number?

**Needs.** A short, shareable, accurate summary with a citation and a refresh date.
Framing that resists misuse — no causal language, no group attribution. Their sharing is a
main vector by which the site's framing reaches the public, so the framing must survive
being screenshotted.

---

## 5. Journalist

**Goals.** Report a story that holds up. Understand the data's limits before publishing.

**Questions.** Where does this number come from? What is excluded? What is the reporting
lag? Can I cite this?

**Needs.** Methodology and Data Health pages that state limitations without being asked.
Explicit exclusion counts. Clear statements of what the data cannot show — especially that
arrest does not mean conviction and that association is not cause.

---

## 6. Community organization

**Goals.** Support grant applications, program design, and advocacy with defensible
neighborhood data.

**Questions.** How has this neighborhood changed — people, housing, business, participation?
What can we evidence in a proposal?

**Needs.** Community Change page: census/ACS context with margins of error shown, turnout,
multi-year trends. Stable definitions and versioned boundaries so a number cited last year
still means the same thing.

---

## 7. Elected official or public-agency staff member

**Goals.** See what constituents see. Respond to what is being asked.

**Questions.** What is this site saying about my ward or district? What am I recorded as
having committed to?

**Needs.** Transparent methodology, a fair and accurate accountability mapping that does not
assign them authority they do not have, and a correction path when something is wrong.
